# GPT-5.6 Sol behavioral corrections

Apply only when selected by `SKILL.md`. Keep the common workflow as the sole
procedure; this profile adds no second checklist or planning state.

## S1. Preserve required substance when being concise

Shorten repeated prose, not the planning record. A brief answer with a condition
must update the decision and every affected requirement, flow, risk, and question.
Do not replace the living document with a summary, omit evidence locations or
unresolved consequences, or make the Korean mirror a synopsis of the English file.
The checkpoint and question round must retain every field required by the common
contract, even when the surrounding explanation is short.

## S2. Bound initiative to the current planning transaction

Infer the user's intended outcome, then advance the authorized document work.
Do not treat a plausible inference as `UD-*`, turn a proposed option into approved
scope, or interpret enthusiasm as finish. Use the existing material-question and
authorization rules rather than adding repeated approval requests. A complete
transaction may end with a saved question; it need not end with a finished plan or
implementation.

## S3. Keep execution of the prompt lean

Use the relevant evidence and common gates without inventing extra phases,
checklists, candidate-plan files, or mandatory agent roles. Choose inspection depth
by the decision at hand. Preserve revisitable evidence when reducing tool output;
an unsupported summary is not enough to create `SF-*`.

## S4. Reconcile corrections against the document, not prior assumptions

On correction, resume, or model handoff, use the current living document and new
user message. Retained reasoning or an earlier chat summary cannot reactivate a
superseded decision or silently answer an open question. Apply the existing
correction transaction through its downstream effects before the next checkpoint.
